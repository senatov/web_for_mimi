Features

A focused radar for the live trading session

MiMiTrends narrows a broad rotating universe into two readable event types without presenting either one as a recommendation.

01

Rapid live crashes

Detect a 0.30% four-minute or 0.60% sustained fifteen-minute decline, subject to price, volume, turnover, and data-continuity checks. The table shows RAPID CRASH in red on a light-yellow cell and moves it to the independent priority scanner.

02

Tradable corridors

Find stable two-hour ranges with repeated edge touches, bounded drift, and enough remaining room to be worth inspecting.

03

Broad liquid rotation

Prioritize recent turnover and feed freshness, process up to 90 eligible symbols per regular cycle, and rotate through larger US and European universes.

04

Selective external sources

See each source's latest activity beside the radar. Scalable can refresh accepted signals when an ISIN and valid login are available; optional venue adapters remain configurable.

FAQ

Know what the scanner does—and what it does not

Is MiMiTrends a trading bot?

No. It is informational software: it does not place orders, provide investment advice, or guarantee that a detected anomaly will continue.

Which markets are supported?

The radar supports configurable US, European, or combined watchlists. It processes up to 90 eligible symbols per regular cycle, rotates through larger universes, and adds at most 20 public discovery candidates every 30 minutes.

What does RAPID CRASH mean?

Confirmed closing prices declined by at least 0.30% over four minutes or 0.60% over a sustained fifteen-minute window, subject to price, volume, turnover, and continuity checks. The event is an alert, not a forecast or trading advice.

What else does the radar publish?

TRADABLE CORRIDOR marks a stable two-hour range with repeated edge interaction, limited drift, and usable remaining room. Deep trend, prediction, and long-horizon research models are outside the active radar.

Do I need an API key?

No key is required for the default data path. Finnhub is an optional enhancement for supported US symbols.

Where is my data stored?

Minute history, company profiles, compact scan runs, accepted corridor/crash events, preferences, and imported executions are stored locally in SQLite. Retention and backup cleanup run during startup and shutdown.

Which platforms are ready?

The application is cross-platform by design. The current UI and builds have been tested on macOS; Windows and Linux packages are implemented but not yet platform-verified.

How is it built?

MiMiTrends is a modular Kotlin/JVM application with a JavaFX desktop interface and Gradle-based native packaging.
