Features

A focused radar for the live trading session

MiMiTrends turns a broad rotating universe into readable corridor, rapid-rise, and rapid-crash alerts. Each result is information for review, not a trading recommendation.

trending_up

Rapid rises

Detect a rise from a recent close to a minute-candle high. The default is 1.5% within four minutes; price, volume, turnover, continuity, and a preceding-high check filter weak moves.

trending_down

Rapid crashes

Detect a 0.30% four-minute or 0.60% sustained fifteen-minute decline with market-data quality checks. Active crashes receive independent priority checks.

show_chart

Two-hour corridors

Find stable ranges with repeated edge touches, bounded drift, and remaining room to inspect. Alerts show measured movement, price, and age.

radar

Liquid market rotation

Rank by turnover and feed freshness, scan up to 90 eligible instruments per regular cycle, and rotate US and European watchlists with bounded public discovery.

sensors

Source activity

Inspect the latest operation, age, and valid item counts for each source. Optional Scalable and venue adapters remain separate from the default data path.

candlestick_chart

Signal charts

Inspect minute candles and volume in focused or full-history views, with optional overlays for imported executions.

upload_file

Local CSV import

Import completed broker transactions into local SQLite and match them to chart intervals without uploading portfolio data.

article

Coverage and headlines

Open the activity-ranked universe and recent linked headlines on demand. Headlines are reading leads, not scanner-confirmed explanations.

FAQ

Know what the scanner does—and what it does not

Is MiMiTrends a trading bot?

No. It is informational software: it does not place orders, provide investment advice, or guarantee that a detected anomaly will continue.

Which markets are supported?

The radar supports configurable US, European, or combined watchlists. It processes up to 90 eligible symbols per regular cycle, rotates through larger universes, and adds at most 40 public discovery candidates every 30 minutes.

What do rapid moves mean?

RAPID CRASH starts at a 0.30% four-minute or 0.60% sustained fifteen-minute decline by default. RAPID RISE starts at a 1.5% rise within four minutes by default. Both require market-data quality checks; thresholds are editable in Settings. These are alerts, not forecasts.

What else does the radar publish?

TRADABLE CORRIDOR marks a stable two-hour range with repeated edge interaction, limited drift, and usable remaining room. Deep trend, prediction, and long-horizon research models are outside the active radar.

Do I need an API key?

No key is required for the default data path. Finnhub is an optional enhancement for supported US symbols.

Where is my data stored?

Minute history, company profiles, compact scan runs, accepted events, preferences, and imported executions are stored locally in SQLite. Retention and backup cleanup run during startup and shutdown.

Which platforms are ready?

The application is cross-platform by design. The current UI and builds have been tested on macOS; Windows and Linux packages are implemented but not yet platform-verified.

How is it built?

MiMiTrends is a modular Kotlin/JVM application with a JavaFX desktop interface and Gradle-based native packaging.
